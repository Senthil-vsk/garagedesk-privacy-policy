Privacy Policy for GarageDesk

Last updated: August 30, 2026

GarageDesk is a vehicle service and workshop management application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information entered into the application.

Information Entered in the Application

Users may enter and manage information such as customer details, vehicle information, service records, billing information, spare parts inventory, and other workshop-related data.

Data Storage

GarageDesk stores application data locally on the user's device. The application does not use our own cloud servers to store or process your data.

Data Collection and Sharing

GarageDesk does not collect, transmit, sell, or share the data entered into the application with us or third parties.

Internet Access

The core functionality of GarageDesk is designed to work offline. Your workshop and customer data is not uploaded to our servers.

Data Backup and Responsibility

Because data is stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for maintaining backups of their data where backup or export functionality is available. Uninstalling the application, clearing application data, resetting the device, or losing the device may result in the loss of locally stored information.

Data Security

We recommend that users protect their devices using appropriate device security measures, such as screen locks or passwords. Since data is stored locally, the security of the device may affect the security of the information stored by the application.

Children's Privacy

GarageDesk is intended for workshop and business management purposes and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
