Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

GarageDesk is a vehicle service and workshop management application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information entered into the application.

Offline-First Architecture: GarageDesk operates with an offline-first model. We do not store, process, or harvest your business records on external cloud servers.

1. Information Entered in the Application

Users may enter and manage information necessary for workshop operations, such as customer contact details, vehicle information, service and repair records, billing data, spare parts inventory, and related operational data.

2. Data Storage

GarageDesk stores application data locally on your device. The application does not use external or developer-owned cloud servers to store, sync, or process your operational data.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

GarageDesk does not collect, transmit, sell, monetize, or share the data entered into the application with us or any third parties.

4. Internet Access & Network Usage

The core functionality of GarageDesk is designed to work fully offline. Your workshop and customer records are never uploaded to our servers.

5. Data Backup and User Responsibility

Because all records remain strictly local to your device, users bear sole responsibility for generating and safeguarding backups using available export or backup tools. Uninstalling the application, clearing app storage, performing device factory resets, or losing the device will result in the permanent loss of locally stored data.

6. Device Security

We strongly advise users to maintain device-level security controls, including PINs, biometric locks, or passwords. Because data remains locally stored, the overall confidentiality of your business records relies directly on your device security posture.

7. Children's Privacy

GarageDesk is designed strictly for business management and workshop operations. It is not directed to or intended for use by children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be reflected directly within this document along with an updated effective revision date.
